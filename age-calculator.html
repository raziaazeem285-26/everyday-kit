How to use the age calculator

How it works

Enter your date of birth. The calculator compares it with today (or any date you choose in the second field) using real calendar months, so leap years and varying month lengths are handled correctly.

Privacy

Your date of birth never leaves your device — the calculation runs entirely in your browser.

Example

Born June 15, 1990 → on October 18, 2026 you are 36 years, 4 months, and 3 days old. That is 13,274 days in total.

Frequently asked questions

How is age in months and days calculated?

We compare calendar dates directly: full years first, then remaining months, then remaining days &mdash; accounting for leap years and different month lengths.

Can I find someone's age on a past date?

Yes. Enter any date in the "Age at this date" field to see how old someone was (or will be) on that day.

Why does an online age calculator differ by a day sometimes?

Time zones and whether the start day is counted can shift results by a day. We count using calendar dates at midnight, the standard convention.
