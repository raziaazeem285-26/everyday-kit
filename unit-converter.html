How to use the unit converter

Four categories

Length: meters, kilometers, centimeters, millimeters, miles, yards, feet, inches.

Weight: kilograms, grams, tonnes, pounds, ounces.

Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin.

Volume: liters, milliliters, US gallons, fluid ounces, cups.

Accuracy

Conversions use internationally defined factors (e.g. 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly). Results update live as you type.

Example

1 kilogram = 2.20462 pounds. Select Weight, enter 1, choose kg → lb.

Frequently asked questions

How many pounds is 1 kilogram?

1 kilogram equals 2.20462 pounds (exactly 2.2046226218 lb).

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32. Our converter handles Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin instantly.

Are US or UK gallons used?

US gallons. One US gallon = 3.78541 liters.
