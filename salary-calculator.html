How to use the salary calculator

How it works

Enter your hourly rate, hours worked per week, and weeks worked per year. Annual salary = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year.

Part-time or unpaid leave?

Adjust the "weeks worked per year" field — e.g. 48 weeks if you take 4 unpaid weeks off.

Note on taxes

Results show gross pay before taxes and deductions, which vary by country and state.

Example

$20/hour × 40 hours/week × 52 weeks = $41,600 per year ($3,467/month, $800/week).

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert $20/hour to annual salary?

Multiply by hours per week and weeks per year: $20 × 40 × 52 = $41,600 per year.

Is this before or after tax?

Before tax (gross). Net take-home pay depends on your local tax rates and deductions.

Can I use it for part-time work?

Yes &mdash; just enter your actual weekly hours and weeks worked.
