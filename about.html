About Us

What is EverydayKit?

EverydayKit is a collection of free online tools for the calculations people do every day: working out a percentage discount, finding an exact age, counting days between dates, converting an hourly wage to a salary, or switching between measurement units.

Why we built it

We believe simple tools should be simple to use. No accounts, no downloads, no paywalls — just type a number and get a trustworthy answer, on any device.

Our promise

Free, always. Every tool on this site is free to use.
Private by design. Calculations run in your browser; we don't ask for personal information to use the tools.
Accurate. Each calculator is tested against known values before release.

What's next

We plan to add more everyday tools over time — from finance helpers to health calculators. If there's a tool you'd like to see, let us know.
