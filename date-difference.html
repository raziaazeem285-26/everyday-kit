How to use the date difference calculator

How days are counted

We count calendar days between the two dates, not including the start date. So January 1 to January 11 is 10 days — the standard convention used by most date calculators.

Order does not matter

You can enter the dates in either order; the tool shows the absolute difference and tells you if the end date comes first.

Example

January 1, 2026 to January 11, 2026 = 10 days (1 week and 3 days).

Frequently asked questions

How many days are between January 1 and January 11?

10 days. The start date is not counted; each following calendar day adds one.

Does the calculator handle leap years?

Yes. Dates are compared as real calendar dates, so February 29 in leap years is handled correctly.

Can I count months or years too?

Yes &mdash; the result also shows the equivalent in weeks, approximate months, and years.
