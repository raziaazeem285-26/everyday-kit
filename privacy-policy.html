Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Summary

EverydayKit's tools run entirely in your web browser. We do not require accounts, and we do not collect the numbers or dates you enter into the calculators.

Information we collect

Tool inputs: never sent to us — all calculations happen on your device.
Contact form: the form composes a message on your device; nothing is transmitted until you choose to send it yourself.
Server logs: our hosting provider may log basic technical data (such as IP address and pages visited) to keep the site secure and reliable.

Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Vercel, which processes technical data as described in its own privacy policy. We do not sell personal information to anyone.

Children

EverydayKit is a general-audience utility site and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via the contact page.
