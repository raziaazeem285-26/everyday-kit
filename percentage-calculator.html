How to use the percentage calculator

Three modes in one tool

What is X% of Y? — e.g. a 20% discount on a $150 item.

X is what % of Y? — e.g. 30 correct answers out of 150 questions.

Percentage increase / decrease — e.g. how much a price rose from $80 to $100.

Tips

Results update live as you type. Decimals are fine — try 12.5% of 240.

Example

20% of 150 = 30. If a store offers 20% off a $150 jacket, you save $30 and pay $120.

Frequently asked questions

What is 20% of 150?

20% of 150 equals 30. Multiply 150 by 0.20.

How do I find percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100. Our tool does this instantly.

Can I use decimals?

Yes &mdash; the calculator accepts decimal numbers in every field.
